Plan Your Visit

Welcome to HAAC

Thinking about visiting? We'd be honored to have you. Here's everything you need to feel at home on your first Sunday.

Come as you are. Whether it's your first time in a church or you've been walking with Jesus for years, there's a seat for you here.

What to expect

  • 1Arrive a few minutes early. Friendly greeters will help you find your way and get your children checked in.
  • 2Worship with us in Hmong and English. There's no dress code — come comfortable.
  • 3Stay afterward for coffee and fellowship so we can welcome you personally.

What about my kids?

Children are always welcome in the service, and our Children's Ministry offers safe, joyful, age-appropriate classes during worship. Check-in takes just a minute at the welcome table.

How long is the service?

Plan for about 90 minutes of worship, teaching, and prayer, followed by time to connect.

Our Church

About HAAC

A Christ-centered church striving to be the light to those who are in darkness.

Our mission

HAAC exists to worship the Lord, nurture believers, equip them for ministry, and evangelize the lost.

Our story

The Hmong American Alliance Church is a family of believers in Maplewood, Minnesota, rooted in the gospel and shaped by our Hmong heritage. We gather across generations and languages to know Christ and make Him known.

Be The Light

Our heart is captured in a simple calling — to be the light of Christ to those around us, in our homes, our community, and to the ends of the earth.
